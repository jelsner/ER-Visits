We replaced ZIP-only confidence intervals with a two-way ZIP-and-season bootstrap, independently resampling ZIPs and entire May–November seasons and refitting both model components with identical product weights in each of 999 draws per intensity threshold. Point estimates were unchanged, but intervals widened: Impact-phase reductions remained below zero at every threshold, Threat-phase intervals included zero throughout, and Cleanup-phase intervals excluded zero only at ≥64 kt; we have revised the presentation accordingly and acknowledge the limited number of independent hurricane seasons.
