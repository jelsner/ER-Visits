For the ≥34 kt analysis, we refitted both model components after individually excluding each of 39 storms and each of 18 May–November seasons. Storm exclusions removed all exposed ZIP-days from day −8 through +8, and season exclusions removed all ZIP-days in the omitted season. The Impact estimate remained negative in every refit, ranging from −1.79 to −1.20 visits per ZIP-day across storm omissions and from −1.68 to −1.17 across season omissions, compared with −1.50 in the full sample. Thus, no single storm or season accounted for the direction of the Impact association. Threat and Cleanup point estimates also remained positive, although the Threat estimate fell from 0.29 to 0.04 when 2022 was excluded. These are descriptive influence checks with predictions standardized over each retained sample; uncertainty is assessed using the full-sample two-way ZIP-and-season bootstrap.
