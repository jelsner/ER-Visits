We assessed sensitivity to exposure width by multiplying each retained segment's RMW by 1.5 while holding the original storm inventory, intensity classifications and dates fixed. Impact estimates were -1.65, -2.91 and -3.95 visits per ZIP-day at ≥34, ≥55 and ≥64 kt, respectively, compared with -1.50, -2.86 and -3.96 under the original RMW definition; Threat and Cleanup estimates moved closer to zero at all three thresholds.
